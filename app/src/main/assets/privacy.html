Privacy Policy


Welcome to SafeKids. This Privacy Policy is written by the SafeKids development group — a team of Computer Science final year students at the University of Gondar. It explains in detail how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our application. SafeKids is designed with child safety and parental supervision in mind, so handling data responsibly is a core part of how the system works. By using the application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Introduction and Applicability of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of SafeKids, including parents, guardians, and children whose devices are connected to the system.
We created this policy to be as clear and transparent as possible so users can understand what happens to their data when they use the application. Since SafeKids is a monitoring and digital wellbeing tool, it naturally interacts with sensitive device information, and we take that responsibility seriously.
This Policy applies whenever you:
- Install or use the SafeKids application
- Create or manage an account
- Connect a child device to the system
- Use any features of the Service
By continuing to use SafeKids, you confirm that you understand and agree to how data is handled as described here.

2. Data We Collect and Receive

To make SafeKids work properly, we may collect different types of information depending on how the app is used. We try to limit collection only to what is necessary for core functionality.
The types of data we may collect include:
- Device Information: such as device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and system settings
- Application Usage Data: including screen time, app usage duration, frequently used apps, and activity patterns
- Activity Logs: basic logs that help generate reports and show how the device is being used
- Location Data: only when the parent or guardian explicitly enables location tracking
- Notification Data: information about notifications received on the device, when permission is granted
- Account Information: such as email address or login credentials used to create and manage accounts

We do not collect unnecessary personal data, and we do not access unrelated files, photos, or private messages unless explicitly required for a feature and enabled by the parent.

3. Children’s Personal Data

SafeKids is designed specifically for parents and guardians to supervise children’s device usage in a responsible way.
We do not allow children to independently manage or control sensitive privacy settings inside the application.
All child-related data is collected and managed under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian who has full control of the account.
We strongly encourage parents to:
- Explain the purpose of SafeKids to their children in an age-appropriate way
- Use monitoring features responsibly and ethically
- Ensure that children understand this is for safety, not punishment or misuse
We do not intentionally collect data directly from children without parental involvement.

4. How We Use Data

The data collected through SafeKids is used strictly to support the functionality of the application and improve child safety.
We use data for purposes such as:
- Providing screen time monitoring and usage reports
- Helping parents understand device activity patterns
- Enabling location tracking and safety alerts when activated
- Improving system performance and fixing technical issues
- Ensuring the security and stability of the application
We do not use personal data for advertising, marketing, or selling purposes. Our focus is only on safety, education, and system functionality.

5. Sharing of Your Personal Data

We respect user privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data.
Your data is only shared in very limited cases such as:
- When required by applicable laws or legal authorities
- When necessary to protect the security or integrity of the system
- Between parent and child accounts within the SafeKids system
Other than these cases, all data remains within the SafeKids environment and is not shared externally with third-party companies or advertisers.

6. How We Protect Your Data

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
These measures include:
- Limiting access to sensitive data only to authorized system components
- Using secure storage methods for collected information
- Applying internal safeguards to reduce risk of data leaks
- Monitoring system behavior to detect unusual or harmful activity
Although we work hard to protect all data, it is important to understand that no digital system can guarantee complete security. Users are also responsible for keeping their accounts safe.

7. Storing of Your and Your Child’s Data

We store data only for as long as it is needed to provide SafeKids services effectively.
Stored data helps us:
- Maintain monitoring history and reports
- Provide accurate usage statistics
- Improve user experience and system performance
Data may be deleted, updated, or anonymized when it is no longer needed or when the account is removed. Parents may also manage certain data through account settings depending on available features.

8. Your Rights and Options

We believe users should have control over their data and how SafeKids operates.
As a user (especially as a parent or guardian), you may:
- Access and review your account information at any time
- Update or correct your personal details
- Enable or disable certain features such as location tracking
- Control monitoring settings for child devices
- Stop using the Service whenever you choose
For child devices, all decisions are controlled by the parent or guardian. We encourage users to regularly review settings to ensure they match their preferences and family rules.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

As SafeKids continues to improve, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When changes are made:
- We will update the “Last Updated” date
- The revised version will be available inside the application
- Important changes may be highlighted to users where necessary
We encourage users to review this Policy occasionally to stay informed about how their data is handled.
Continuing to use SafeKids after updates means you accept the revised Privacy Policy.
